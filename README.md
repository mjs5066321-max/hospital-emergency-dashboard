📊 Hospital Emergency Room Dashboard – April 2024
This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze and monitor emergency room operations. The dashboard provides a monthly view of key performance metrics, patient demographics, and departmental referrals, helping hospital administrators improve efficiency and patient care.

🔹**Key Features**
Patient Volume Tracking: 5K patients in April 2024, with daily and hourly breakdowns to identify peak load periods.

Operational KPIs: Average wait time (35 minutes), patient satisfaction score (4.94), and referral counts (2K).

Admission Status: Comparison of admitted vs. non‑admitted patients.

Service Efficiency: Percentage of patients seen within 30 minutes (59.44% met target).

Demographics: Age group distribution, gender split, and racial breakdown.

Department Referrals: Insights into referrals across General Practice, Orthopedics, Cardiology, Neurology, and more.

🔹 **Tools & Technologies**
Power BI (data modeling, DAX measures, interactive visuals)

Excel / Power Query (data cleaning and transformation)

Data Sources: Hospital ER records (simulated dataset)

🔹 **Insights Delivered**
Identified peak patient load hours to optimize staffing.

Highlighted wait time bottlenecks and satisfaction trends.

Provided actionable insights into referral patterns across departments.

Supported workforce planning by analyzing demographics and admission status.
